# 0005. Vocab deck of 1,500 words, 200 new a day, then Round 2
2026-10-31 · Accepted · Planning · Scope

## Context
The first plan was a 1,000-word deck at 50 new words a day. With the exam fixed on Nov 24 and study starting Nov 1, 50 a day would cover only about 1,000 words once, with little time left for review. The deck size and daily pace had to fit the calendar.

## Options
- **1,000 words, 50 a day:** gentle pace; about 20 days to see the deck once, almost no second pass.
- **1,500 words, 200 a day, then Round 2:** whole deck seen in about 8 days, then repeated passes weakest-first until the exam.
- **No cap:** fastest exposure, but review load grows faster than users can clear it.

## Decision
The 1,500 most frequent words, a default cap of 200 new words per local day (changeable in Settings), due reviews before new words, and compressed Leitner intervals (same session, 1, 3, 7 days, retired) to suit a 3-week window. Once every word has been seen, the app offers Round 2, ordered starred, then box 1, up to retired.

## Trade-offs
200 new words a day is a heavy load: about four 50-card sessions plus reviews. Some words will be seen without being learned on the first pass, and Round 2 is designed to catch those. Round 2 itself is Phase 2, so it has to ship within the first ~8 days of use.

## Signals to revisit
- Due reviews pile up faster than a user can clear them in a day.
- Known/Retired counts stall well below the 1,200-word target.
- Either user lowers the cap in Settings and keeps it lower.
