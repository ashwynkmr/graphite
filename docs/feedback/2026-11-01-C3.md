# Feedback: push reminders
2026-11-01 · C3 · Staging with a temporary server, on a real iPhone

## What was tested
A fixed daily reminder, then usage-based reminders, on staging with the push server running on Ashwyn's Mac through a tunnel. Before that, Chromium ran the update from the live build, offline use, and the full chain through Google's push service. The streak and time-away rules are covered by unit tests only, since they depend on the time of day or days passing. Android is not yet tested. The final round ran on staging against the live service on Cloudflare.

## What we deliberately didn't change
- Results stay on the device. The server receives a push address and wake-up times only.
- Not yet known: whether the reminders feel helpful or naggy over several days.

## Found in testing, before review
- A paused session would have been nudged repeatedly, and the daily limit ignored reminders already sent.
