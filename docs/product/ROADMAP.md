# Roadmap

Last updated 2026-10-31. Study starts Nov 1; exam Nov 24, 2026. Phase scope is defined in the [PRD](PRD.md#scope-and-release-phases); changes to it are recorded below and in the [decision log](../decisions/README.md).

## Phase 1: launch (target Nov 1–2)

Built in three milestones so studying can start before all of Phase 1 is done ([0014](../decisions/0014-quant-drill-before-handwritten-problems.md)).

| Milestone | Steps | Target | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A. Quant drills live** (`v0.1.0`) | 1 Scaffold + deploy · 2 Engine · 3 Generators + ~1,200-question bank · 4 Data layer · 5 Quant flow + minimal Home | Nov 1 | In progress (step 1) |
| **B. Vocab live** (`v0.2.0`) | 6 Ranked word list · 7 Entries for ranks 1–600 · 8 Vocab flow | Nov 1–2 | Not started |
| **C. Rest of Phase 1** (`v1.0.0`) | 9 Mistake log, full Home, Progress, Settings, Share · 10 ~300 hand-written problems (bank → ~1,500) · 11 Launch pass | Nov 2 | Not started |

## Phase 2: first week (by Nov 7)

Starts only when Ashwyn says go.

- Words 601–1,500 in batches of 300
- Sprint mode and Adaptive difficulty
- Round 2, Browse and search
- Full dashboard: 14-day trends, weakest topics, words per box
- Share 7- or 14-day history
- Mnemonics for the hardest ~200 words; type-in hint toggle; per-topic defaults; mistake-log filters

## Phase 3: later (optional)

Quantitative comparison and data-interpretation questions, audio pronunciation, score predictor, compare with a friend (needs a free backend), Android APK.
