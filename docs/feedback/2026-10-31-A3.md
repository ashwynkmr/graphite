## Lessons
- "It doesn't work" on staging can mean a stale build. Each review now starts by checking that the phone has loaded the newest version, and the update bar is visible from any tab.

## What we deliberately didn't change
- The drill and results flow stays as built (FB-13).
- Difficulty definitions stay as recalibrated in [0017](../decisions/0017-difficulty-up-one-level.md); the multi-select only changes which levels are mixed into a drill.
